4 Tips for Creating Secure Passwords

  1. Use long passwords: Aim for at least 15 characters. A memorable passphrase is a good option.
  2. Use unique passwords: Never reuse school passwords on personal accounts.
  3. Use a password manager: Choose one approved by your school's IT department.
  4. Enable MFA: Add multi-factor authentication whenever available.

Remember: This website does not collect or analyze passwords.
