Four password safety tips

  1. Choose length: Use a long, memorable password or passphrase; aim for at least 15 characters.
  2. Keep passwords unique: Don't reuse school credentials on personal accounts.
  3. Use a password manager: Choose a tool approved by your school IT team.
  4. Enable MFA: Turn on multi-factor authentication whenever possible.
